GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trading with MacroLink. Can't find an answer? Reach out directly.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard MOQ is 5 Metric Tons per product per shipment. For bulk orders above 20 MT we offer special pricing. We can also accommodate mixed-container loads with multiple produce types.

Which ports do you ship from?

We primarily ship from Mundra Port and Nhava Sheva (JNPT). We also handle shipments through Kandla, Chennai, and Kolkata ports depending on the destination.

What documents do you provide with each shipment?

We provide: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, Quality Certificate, and all APEDA/DGFT regulatory documents required by the destination country.

What payment terms do you accept?

We accept TT (Telegraphic Transfer), LC (Letter of Credit), and advance payment. For new buyers, we typically require 30–50% advance with the balance against shipping documents.

Can you arrange product samples before a bulk order?

Yes. We can arrange samples for quality evaluation via air freight before committing to a bulk order. Contact us to discuss sample quantities, costs, and shipping arrangements.

How long does delivery take to reach my country?

Approximate transit times: Middle East 5–10 days · Africa 12–22 days · SE Asia 10–18 days · Europe 18–28 days · Americas 25–35 days. All produce ships in temperature-controlled reefer containers.
