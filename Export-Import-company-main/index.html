Founded in 2025, MacroLink Exports and Imports was built on a simple yet powerful idea — connecting the world through quality trade. GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trading with MacroLink. Can't find an answer? Reach out directly.

Which ports do you ship from?

We primarily ship from Mundra Port, Nhava Sheva (JNPT), and V. O. Chidambaranar Port Trust, Tuticorin.

What documents do you provide with each shipment?

We provide: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, Quality Certificate, and all APEDA/DGFT regulatory documents required by the destination country.

What payment terms do you accept?

We accept TT (Telegraphic Transfer), LC (Letter of Credit), and advance payment. For new buyers, we typically require 50–70% advance with the balance against shipping documents.

Can you arrange product samples before a bulk order?

Yes. We can arrange samples for quality evaluation via air freight before committing to a bulk order. Contact us to discuss sample quantities, costs, and shipping arrangements.

How long does delivery take to reach my country?

Approximate transit times: Middle East 5–10 days · SE Asia 10–18 days · Europe 18–28 days · Americas 25–35 days. All produce ships in temperature-controlled reefer containers.
